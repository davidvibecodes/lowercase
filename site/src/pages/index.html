Questions, answered

How is this different from a casebook or a case library?

A casebook gives you finished answers to read. lowercase makes you produce the thinking yourself: you learn a concept, prove you can use it, then lead a case where facts arrive only when you ask for them. Your progress decides what comes next.

I've never done a case interview. Where do I start?

At Level 1. The first lessons cover the profit equation, spotting the case type and asking good clarifying questions. You'll be running a simple case with a friend within your first session.

Do I need a friend to use lowercase?

No. Concept lessons and review work on your own, and the AI interviewer (on Marathon and Journey) gives you full cases without a partner. Practising with friends adds the pressure of a real person, so we recommend mixing both.

Is the AI interviewer available today?

It's in early access. Marathon members get the chat interviewer and Journey members get chat and voice as they roll out. Practising with friends and every concept lesson are available on all plans now.

What happens when my plan ends?

Nothing renews automatically. Plans are one-time passes, because the goal is for you to be ready, not to stay subscribed.
