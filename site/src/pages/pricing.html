Pricing questions

Which plan should I choose?

If your interviews are a month away and you have a practice partner, Sprint. If you're starting from scratch or want an AI interviewer between sessions, Marathon. If you want the full programme and to practise speaking your answers out loud, Journey.

Will I be charged again when my plan ends?

No. Every plan is a one-time payment. Your access simply ends on the last day. If you want more time you can buy another pass, but we'd rather you didn't need it.

What does "early access" mean for the AI interviewer?

The AI interviewer is rolling out now. Marathon and Journey members get it as it becomes available, with voice coming to Journey. Everything else on each plan works today.
